Cross Contamination

Cross Contamination is a PS1 styled melee beat'em up where you take control of a half-mutated brawler named Lucan Cross, using your empowering vials to fight through a mutant-infested city to try and find a cure!

As the PROGRAMMER & LEAD for this Project, It is my job to take care of everything from Programming, Creating the Build, Planning Gameplay, and More!

Below is my Development Process where I post updates on progress of the project, what I did, how I did it, and what I learned from my experience.

Status: In Development

Development Updates & Devlog

Devlog #1

This marks my first Devlog Entry for the game, I'd originally wanted to hold off until the game was published to steam as a Demo, but I figured I should probably just get around to it already.

This game was originally a game I worked on for the Global Game Jam 2026, Escape From Chicago, the group I worked on that project with all decided that we liked the basis of our idea and we all meshed well together so we decided to continue working together and take the idea to the next level!

We started by putting more effort and refinement into existing systems, with our character designer completely remaking the main character and all of the animations to go along with it, as well as doing the same for the enemies, I followed suit by remaking my existing systems, including the vial system and movement to work completely differently, basically starting from scratch!

Our goal was to get a working demo of the game done by the end of the current semester, and while we didn't fully achieve this goal, we got very close, and after a little bit of bugfixing and testing, everything will be ready! There was quite a lot of new things I created for the demo, such as Enemy Finishers, Dialogue, and even a saving system based off of how saves worked on the PS2!

Rammer Finisher:

As you can see in the video above, I also got new enemy types working, we settled on having 3 enemy types in total, the original that we had in the Game Jam Version, with a remade model and animations of course, as well as two new enemies, The Rammer and The Snatcher, all three of which differed greatly from each other in terms of programming and gameplay!

Base Enemy and Snatcher Code:

While the original enemy we had was just a basic one that would run at you and attack you, these new types functioned differently, The Rammer roars and charges at you from a distance, getting stunned briefly after each ram, while The Snatcher is a small bug that will come up to you and attempt to latch on to you, blocking you from hitting other enemies if it is successful! All of these were pretty difficult to program but all game out really well and help to take the combat from being a mindless hitfest to actually having to plan things

Along with the new enemies, I have also added a finisher system, which was shown in the video above, allowing you initiate instant-kill finishers if you manage to stun an enemy correctly, which incorporate Quick Time Events inspired by the original God of War games in order to keep players ingaged when using finishers, instead of it being a one-button instant kill!

Second Finisher Type:

Another thing I worked on that I was really proud of it the intro screen and cutscenes, which serve mostly to begin and end the demo level, and I think came out really well, it took a lot of work wasn't easy to get done the first time, causeing me to go through multiple variations, but eventually I found a version I was happy with!

Intro Cutscene:
